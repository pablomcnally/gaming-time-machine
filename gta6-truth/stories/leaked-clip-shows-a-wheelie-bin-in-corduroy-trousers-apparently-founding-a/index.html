A wildly unverified clip has set the internet wobbling after allegedly showing a wheelie bin in proper corduroys taking over a civic office and sorting crisps with...

The footage, if it can be called that without upsetting the camera, appears to show a determined wheelie bin rolling into a municipal corridor like it owns the lease and the biscuits.
