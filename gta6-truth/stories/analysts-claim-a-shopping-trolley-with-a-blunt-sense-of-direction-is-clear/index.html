Analysts Claim A Shopping Trolley With A Blunt Sense Of Direction Is Clearly Entering Its

Experts have bravely interpreted an ordinary trolley as a vessel for ambition, regret and possibly loose change.

By P. Underhill, Investigations & Snacks Tuesday, July 7, 2026 4 min read

Following yesterday’s very ordinary sighting of a supermarket trolley drifting across a car park like it had missed the last bus, analysts have issued a bold conclusion: it is absolutely planning something.

According to the panel, the trolley’s one stubborn wheel suggests not a fault, but a "character edge" — the sort usually reserved for old boxers and detective dramas.

The theory holds that the trolley’s route was less accidental and more a "journey of self-discovery", although it still ended beside a wall after three metres.

One expert said the trolley "gave off strong 'former employee returning to clear the air' energy", which is an astonishing amount of feeling to project onto a receptacle for groceries.

Another noted that the basket section seemed to face the sunlight as if "poised for forgiveness".

As ever, this is not a leak, a clue, or even especially sensible. It is simply what happens when people in glasses are left alone with a dramatic shadow and too much tea.
