Analysts Say A Teapot In A Bowler Hat Is Poised To Completely Reimagine Vice City’s Tram Etiquette

Commentators claim the city’s next great systems overhaul may come from a teapot with a public-facing sense of manners and a suspiciously firm grip on side-eye.

By By Maud Weatherby Wednesday, August 26, 2026 4 min read

After six minutes of squinting at promotional crumbs, analysts have reached an important conclusion: Vice City’s tram etiquette is under threat from a teapot in a bowler hat.

The theory is that this highly polished vessel, seen standing near a platform bench with the confidence of a middle manager on day release, may be signalling a new era of transit-based social order.

According to the report, the teapot’s hat placement suggests authority, while the spout angle indicates “active concern” about passengers who board before allowing others to alight. One analyst went further, calling it “the first object in years to truly understand queue shame.”

The broader prediction is that the entire city may begin behaving more politely around stops, crossings, and snack counters if the teapot’s influence proves real. Critics, however, argue that a teapot cannot possibly enforce etiquette without either arms or a clipboard.

This has not stopped the think pieces. There are already charts. There is a graph involving saucers. Someone has produced a map of “hot beverage authority zones.”

Whether this amounts to a revolution or simply a rather fancy kettle being dramatic remains unclear. Still, if Vice City is due for better tram manners, the kettle-shaped consensus is hard to ignore.
