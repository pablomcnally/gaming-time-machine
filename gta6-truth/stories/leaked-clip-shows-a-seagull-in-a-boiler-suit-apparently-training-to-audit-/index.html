‘Leaked’ Clip Shows A Seagull In A Boiler Suit Apparently Training To Audit Vice City’s Chips

A very serious-looking bird is said to have been spotted standing near a takeaway, holding a clipboard and judging the fryer with all the warmth of a customs officer...

By Mavis Grumble Friday, August 14, 2026 3 min read

According to absolutely nobody official, a grainy clip has surfaced of a seagull in a tiny boiler suit and steel-toe boots waddling up to a chip shop like it owns the franchise.

The bird is allegedly seen peering into a basket of fries, nodding once, and giving what onlookers described as ‘the face of a man who has found one too many soggy chips’.

Fans have, naturally, concluded this means Vice City will soon feature a full municipal chip-audit system, complete with bird-led inspections and a firm stance on undercooked batter.

One expert in nonsense suggested the seagull may be part of a wider ecosystem of characters who turn up looking vaguely overqualified for lunch.

If true, it would be the first time a shortlist has ever been presented in wings.
