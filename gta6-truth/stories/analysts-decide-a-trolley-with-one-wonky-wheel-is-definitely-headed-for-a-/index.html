Analysts Decide A Trolley With One Wonky Wheel Is Definitely Headed For A Personal Journey

Our team of trained guessers has taken one look at a slightly unstable shopping trolley and concluded it is, frankly, carrying emotional baggage.

By By C. Merriweather Monday, July 6, 2026 5 min read

The trolley was reportedly spotted drifting across a supermarket car park with the sort of determination usually reserved for minor underdogs in films about themselves.

Analysts noted the left wheel made a noise like disappointment, which they believe is a strong indicator that the trolley has ‘unfinished business’.

Some experts argued the trolley is searching for something it lost years ago, possibly a screw, possibly confidence, possibly a very specific packet of biscuits.

Others suggested it may simply be defective, but this theory was politely ignored in favour of a more dramatic reading with stronger snack potential.

Either way, the trolley is now being watched closely for any signs of a third-act wobble or a moving speech near the tinned goods aisle.
