Analysts Say A Teacup With Sideburns Could Completely Reshape Vice City’s Park-Bench Diplomacy

Our panel of highly casual thinkers believes the next big force in Vice City politics may be a teacup with a distinguished haircut and a surprising grasp of local...

By By Maureen P. Dabble Wednesday, September 2, 2026 4 min read

A team of analysts, absolutely none of whom are known to have passed a formal exam in anything, has concluded that a teacup sporting sideburns could become the decisive factor in Vice City’s park-bench economy.

Their reasoning is breathtakingly simple: if the teacup can command respect from pigeons, toddlers and one suspiciously well-dressed jogger, it may be able to negotiate premium bench rights in the busiest parts of town.

The report claims the cup’s facial hair-like adornments are 'a clear signal of authority', though one commentator suggested they might simply be bits of tea-stained fluff stuck on the porcelain after an unfortunate biscuit incident.

The same analysis predicts ripple effects across the city, including a rise in informal chess diplomacy, a drop in unlicensed goose whispering, and a troubling increase in people asking for 'the full kettle package'.

Naturally, no one has seen the teacup file paperwork, but that has not stopped the market from behaving as though it owns three park districts and a jazz trio.
