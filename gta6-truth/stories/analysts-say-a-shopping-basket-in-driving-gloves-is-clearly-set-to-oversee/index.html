Analysts Say A Shopping Basket In Driving Gloves Is ‘Clearly’ Set To Oversee Vice City’s Queue

Experts have drawn sweeping conclusions from a basket, a pair of gloves, and absolutely no evidence whatsoever.

By By Moira F. Whimsy Wednesday, August 12, 2026 4 min read

Our imaginary analysts have had a busy afternoon with a grainy clip showing a supermarket basket wearing driving gloves and waiting with unnerving patience outside a corner shop.

From this, they have apparently concluded that Vice City’s queue system is about to undergo a major shake-up, with the basket expected to ‘bring order, fairness and possibly coupons’ to the streets.

The reasoning is, as ever, rock solid in the way a wet paper bag is rock solid. The basket is seen turning slightly to the left, which some interpret as a policy stance, while others think it simply noticed a bin.

One expert quoted in the parody realm said the basket’s posture suggested ‘leadership energy’, which is also what people say when they cannot explain a shopping basket in gloves.

If true, the streets of Vice City may soon become a place where everyone queues properly, nobody cuts in, and the basket gives out stern looks to anyone reaching for the last pastry.
